## Tips for generating

- **Generate the whole script in one take.** Line-by-line generation can drift in tone and voice.
- If a line comes out sounding like فصحى or Egyptian, add `[casual Jordanian accent]` before it, or regenerate.
- If English words sound off, try writing them in Arabic letters instead, e.g. `فيلامنت`, `نوزل`, `سلايسر`.
- Export as **WAV or high-quality MP3**. When you send it back, I can sync the animations to the timing of each scene.
